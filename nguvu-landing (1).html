How It Works
Three steps.
Your gym is live.
01
🏢
List on Amka

Configure your gym name, logo, and brand colours. We create your space on the platform and generate a unique 6-character join code — your gym's fingerprint on Amka.

02
📲
Members Download

Share your join code — print it, WhatsApp it, stick it on the wall. Members download Amka once from the Play Store, enter your code, and the entire app themes to your gym instantly.

03
🚀
Amka Does the Rest

Members get auto-personalized training programs, context-aware daily reminders, nutrition tracking, and progress analytics. You get a dashboard, M-Pesa billing, and retention alerts.

Gym Transfers
Moved house?
Switch gyms.
Keep everything.

Your fitness history belongs to you — not your gym. Transfer to any Amka gym instantly and every workout log, weight entry, and nutrition diary comes with you.

1
Find your new gym
Search nearby, filter by neighborhood, or enter the join code your new gym gave you. Westlands to Kilimani — it's all on Amka.
2
Tap Transfer
One tap in Settings → Change Gym. No forms, no waiting for approval, no cancellation calls. Instant self-service. Max 3 transfers / 30 days
3
App re-themes. History intact.
The app immediately themes to your new gym's colours and logo. All your workout logs, weight history, and nutrition data are fully preserved.
